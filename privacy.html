Overview

lewisiii studio ("we") operates informational websites and small web utilities. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Analytics data: anonymized usage statistics (pages visited, approximate region, device type) via privacy-respecting analytics, used to improve our sites.
  • Contact data: if you email us, we keep your email address and message to respond.

What we do not do

  • No account registration, no tracking cookies for advertising, no sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • Our internal tools process keyword research data from Google APIs for our own planning only; that data is never redistributed.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained in aggregate form. Contact emails are kept as long as needed to handle the inquiry.
