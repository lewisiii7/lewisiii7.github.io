Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 — lewisiii studio ("we", "our")

1. Overview

lewisiii studio operates informational websites and small web utilities, and an internal application ("lewisiii studio Console") used by our staff for keyword research and website performance monitoring. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have.

2. Data we collect

  • Website analytics data: anonymized usage statistics of visitors to our websites (pages visited, approximate region, device type), collected via privacy-respecting analytics to improve our sites.
  • Contact data: if you email us, we retain your email address and message content to respond to your inquiry.
  • Data accessed via Google APIs: our internal application accesses, with OAuth user consent, Google Search Console performance data (queries, impressions, clicks), Google Analytics 4 report data, and Google Ads keyword research data — strictly for Google accounts and properties that we own and operate.

3. How we use data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our websites and internal tools.
  • To perform keyword research and SEO monitoring for our own properties.
  • To respond to inquiries.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use data obtained through Google APIs for any purpose other than operating our own properties, and we do not transfer or disclose it to any third party.
  • We do not use advertising tracking cookies or build user profiles.
  • Our services do not generate, host, or distribute AI-generated imagery of any kind.

5. Data storage, security, and retention

API credentials and retrieved data are stored on access-controlled systems with restricted file permissions and are never committed to public source control. Analytics data is retained in aggregate form only. Contact emails are retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you by contacting us. You may also revoke our application's access to your Google account at any time via your Google account permissions page.
